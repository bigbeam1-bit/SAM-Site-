What SAM does

SAM compares publicly offered prices for products across stores and estimates the total cost, including shipping and sales tax, along with delivery dates. SAM does not sell products. Purchases are made directly with the store, under that store's terms, prices, and return policy.

Prices and estimates

Prices, stock, shipping costs, and delivery dates come from stores and data partners and can change without notice. Sales tax is an estimate based on your ZIP code's state average and may differ from the tax charged at checkout. SAM's flags for suspicious prices are automated and may be wrong. Always confirm the final price and details with the store before you buy.
