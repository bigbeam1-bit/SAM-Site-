How SAM works

01

You search once

Enter a product name or model number and your ZIP code. That's it.

02

SAM checks the stores

It matches the exact same product across stores, so a different color, size, refurbished unit, or accessory never sneaks into the comparison.

03

You get one clear answer

The lowest total price you'll actually pay, why it's the best deal, and the date it should arrive. Tap through to buy from the store.

What makes SAM different

Total price, not sticker price

Shipping and estimated sales tax for your ZIP code are included, so the cheapest-looking price isn't a trap.

Fake deals filtered out

Prices far below every other store, or sudden jumps from a store's own price history, are flagged and hidden. SAM tells you when it skipped one.

Arrival dates you can plan around

See "Arrives Thursday" instead of "ships in 3–7 days", and choose whether faster delivery is worth a few dollars more.

Price history and alerts

See a product's 30-day price range, and watch it with a target price. SAM marks it when the price drops.

Our promises

  • No paid placement, ever.Stores cannot pay to rank higher. Rankings come only from total price, delivery, and how trustworthy the price is.
  • Every price shows when it was checked.You always know how fresh a price is.
  • We show our reasoning.Each top pick comes with one plain sentence explaining why it won.
  • You can tell us when we're wrong.Report a price that was higher at checkout, and that store's trust score goes down for everyone.
  • We don't sell your data.No account is needed to search. See our privacy policy.

How SAM earns money

When you buy something after clicking a store link on SAM, the store may pay SAM a small commission through an affiliate program. It costs you nothing extra and never changes which store SAM ranks first. Read the full affiliate disclosure.
