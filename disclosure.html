Affiliate disclosure

Effective November 1, 2026

SAM is free to use. To keep it that way, SAM takes part in affiliate programs, including programs run through networks such as CJ, Impact, Awin, and the eBay Partner Network, and programs run directly by stores.

What this means for you

Some links on SAM that take you to a store are affiliate links. If you click one and then buy something, the store may pay SAM a small commission. You pay the same price whether or not you use an affiliate link.

Commissions never change our rankings

SAM ranks offers only by:

  • the total price you'll pay (item, shipping, and estimated sales tax),
  • delivery time, weighted by your own preference, and
  • how trustworthy the price and store are.

Whether a store pays a commission, and how much, is not part of the ranking. Stores cannot pay for placement. If the best deal is at a store that pays SAM nothing, SAM still shows it first.

Accuracy

Prices, availability, and delivery dates come from stores and data partners and can change at any time. Each price shows when SAM last checked it. Always confirm the final price at checkout. If a price was wrong, please report it in the app.
